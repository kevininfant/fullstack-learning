Round 1 · 20 marks 03
10 bugs

Bug fixing

Eight bugs are identified for you. Two more must be discovered independently.

What a strong submission shows

  • All ten root causes are correctly identified.
  • Each fix restores the expected behavior.
  • Existing functionality remains intact.
  • The final solution is tested before submission.
Accuracy matters more than speculative changes

Assessment integrity 09

Fair play is part of the assessment.

Required conduct

  • Work independently and remain at your assigned workstation.
  • Use only the approved account, machine, browser, editor and resources.
  • Follow all proctor instructions and round timing.
  • Ask a proctor if a requirement or allowed resource is unclear.
  • Stop work immediately when time is called.

Not permitted

  • Talking, messaging, signaling, screen sharing or exchanging files.
  • Viewing, copying or submitting another person's work.
  • Switching browser tabs/windows or opening another application.
  • Searching for solutions or using unapproved websites/resources.
  • Using alternate accounts or attempting to bypass platform controls.
  • Recording, photographing or distributing questions or solutions.
Suspected rule violations may be reviewed by the organizers and can result in submission rejection or disqualification.
